📌 What is Statistics & Course Resources

Statistics is the science of collecting, organizing, analyzing, interpreting, and presenting data. It helps us make sense of numbers and make informed decisions under uncertainty. This field is foundational across all disciplines, from medicine to marketing, and from social sciences to engineering.

⚖️ Ethics & IRB: Institutional Review Boards (IRBs) protect human subjects in research. Core principles: informed consent, confidentiality, minimizing harm, avoiding p-hacking, and pre-registration of studies to reduce bias.
